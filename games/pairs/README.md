# Pairs

A free puzzle game for young kids that works offline. Draw a line with your finger to join each pair of same-coloured dots, and fill every square of the board with the lines. Lines can't cross, so plan the way round. Every level is new, and every level can be solved.

Made for ages 5 and up. There is no timer and no way to lose. Drawing back over a line rubs it out, and Undo takes back the last line.

## Features

- New levels made on your device, in four sizes:
  - **Very easy**: a 4×4 board with 3 pairs
  - **Easy**: a 5×5 board with 4 or 5 pairs
  - **Medium**: a 6×6 board with 5 or 6 pairs
  - **Hard**: a 7×7 board with 6 or 7 pairs
- A level is won when every pair is joined and no square is left empty; any such answer wins, not just one
- Once every pair is joined, any empty squares blink to show what is left
- A ring for each colour that fills in once that pair is joined
- The next level never has the same dots as the one just played
- A count of levels solved at each size
- Picks up where you left off after a reload
- Keeps the screen on while you play
- In English and Croatian, following the phone's language unless you pick one
- Light and dark mode
- No ads, accounts or tracking
