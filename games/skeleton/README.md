# Skeleton

A free puzzle game that works offline. In a haunted house, a skeleton has lost its head. The child swipes the skeleton or its skull, and that one goes the way of the swipe while the other goes like a mirror: the same way up and down, but the other way left and right. Crates, a piano, an old clock, a couch, candlesticks and the walls stop only the one who walks into them. Each of the two can do something the other can't: the small skull rolls under a bed, and the heavy skeleton, standing on a creaky floorboard, swings a bookcase open for the skull to roll through. The skeleton can also push a trunk, and a trunk left on the floorboard holds the bookcase open. On a slippery rug, whoever steps on it slides on until something stops them. When the two meet, the skull hops back on and the skeleton dances.

Made for ages 5 and up. There is no timer and no way to lose.

## Features

- Levels with beds, secret bookcases, trunks and slippery rugs, each a little harder than the one before
- Swipe the skeleton or the skull: the swipe starts on the one it moves
- Every level can be won, no move ever leaves it unwinnable, and the beds, bookcases, trunks and rugs always matter (checked by a test)
- Made for holding the phone upright
- Picks up where you left off after a reload
- Keeps the screen on while you play
- In English and Croatian, following the phone's language unless you pick one
- Light and dark mode
- No ads, accounts or tracking
